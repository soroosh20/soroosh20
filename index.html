Free Program

Program Overview

A free digital safety course for newcomers to Canada.

The Cyber-Safe Core Program (CSCP) is a free online course that teaches newcomers to Canada how to stay safe on the internet. You will learn how to protect yourself, your family, and your personal information online.

This program is funded by Public Safety Canada and delivered by the Immigrant Services Society of British Columbia (ISSofBC). It is designed for people who are new to Canada and may be new to using computers and the internet.

The course is 100% online and completely free. You can take it from home, a library, or anywhere with internet access. You can go at your own pace.

The course is designed for people at CLB 1 to 4 reading levels. The language is simple and clear. Translation support is available.

Course Content

What You Will Learn

The course has three parts. Each part builds on the one before.

Getting Started Online

Layer 1 — Foundation

  • How to use a phone, tablet, or computer safely
  • How to create an email account
  • How to make strong passwords
  • How to connect to the internet safely

Staying Safe Online

Layer 2 — Core Content

  • How to spot fake emails and scam messages
  • How to protect your personal information
  • How to shop online safely
  • How to use social media safely

Your Safety, Your Choice

Layer 3 — Elective Tracks

  • How to keep your children safe online
  • How to use banking apps safely
  • How to spot misinformation and fake news
  • How to report online abuse and cyberbullying
This course is for beginners. You do not need to know computers to start.
